# UniPods Hackathon 2026 — Participant Guidelines

*This is synthetic demo content created for the UniPods Pulse demo. It does not
describe a real event.*

## Key dates

Registration opens on Monday, September 7, 2026.
Team declarations are due Thursday, September 17, 2026, by close of business.
The build period runs from Friday, September 18 to Wednesday, September 23, 2026.
Final submissions close at 23:59 WAT on Wednesday, September 23, 2026.
Demo day is Thursday, September 24, 2026.

## Team rules

Teams must have between two and five members. A participant may belong to only
one team. Team changes after the declaration deadline require written approval
from the organising committee. Solo entries are not accepted this year.

## Judging criteria

Projects are scored out of 100 points across four categories: technical
execution (40 points), product thinking (25 points), demo quality (20 points)
and originality (15 points). Ties are broken on technical execution.

## Submission requirements

Every submission must include a public repository link, a written README, a
three-minute demo video, and a list of every team member with their role.
Submissions missing a demo video are disqualified. Late submissions are not
reviewed under any circumstances.

## Support during the hackathon

Mentor office hours run daily from 16:00 to 18:00 WAT in the community call
room. Technical questions outside those hours go to the #build-help channel.
