# AI Challenge Brief — UniPods Hackathon 2026

*Synthetic demo content for the UniPods Pulse demo.*

## The problem

Community knowledge is scattered across chat messages, call recordings, shared
documents and announcements. Members ask the same questions repeatedly because
finding a previous answer is harder than asking again.

## What we are looking for

Build something that turns scattered community information into answers people
can trust. Submissions are expected to demonstrate retrieval over real
community content rather than a general-purpose chatbot.

## Technical requirements

Entries in the AI track must:

1. Ingest at least two different content types (for example documents and chat
   messages).
2. Return answers that cite the specific source each claim came from.
3. Say clearly when the available information does not support an answer.
4. Run end to end in a live demo, not from a recording.

## Evaluation notes for the AI track

Judges will deliberately ask questions the knowledge base cannot answer. A
system that admits it does not know scores higher than one that guesses.
Hallucinated citations are an automatic fail in this track.

## Data handling

Teams working with private community data must keep it on infrastructure they
control and must delete it after demo day.
