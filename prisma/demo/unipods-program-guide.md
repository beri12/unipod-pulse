# UniPods Program Guide

*Synthetic demo content for the UniPods Pulse demo. Not a real programme

## What UniPods is

UniPods is a community programme that groups members into small "pods" of six
to ten people. Each pod meets weekly, keeps shared notes, and ships one project
per term.

## Membership

Membership runs for one term of twelve weeks. Members are expected to attend at
least eight of the twelve weekly pod calls. Missing four consecutive calls
without notice ends membership for the term.

## Pod leads

Every pod has a lead who runs the weekly call, records decisions in the pod
notes, and raises blockers to the programme team. Pod leads are selected at the
start of each term and serve for the full term.

## Communication

Announcements are posted to the community announcements channel. Day-to-day
discussion happens in pod channels. Anything decided in a call must be written
into the pod notes within 24 hours, otherwise it does not count as decided.

## Resources

Members get access to the shared resource library, the mentor directory, and the
community compute credits pool. Compute credits are requested through the
programme team and are capped at 100 USD per member per term.
